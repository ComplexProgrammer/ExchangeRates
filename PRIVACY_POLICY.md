**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App, and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

The App is designed as an informational and calculation utility providing official currency exchange rates published by the Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan (`cbu.uz`).

### Personal Information
We do **not** require you to create an account, register, or provide any personally identifiable information (such as your name, phone number, address, or banking credentials) to use the App.

### Data Automatically Collected
While using the App, certain non-personally identifiable information may be collected automatically by integrated third-party service providers:
- **Device and Diagnostic Information:** Device model, operating system version, app version, network status, crash reports, and system performance diagnostics (via Firebase Crashlytics).
- **Advertising Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (`AD_ID`), which is used by Google AdMob to serve relevant ads, limit ad frequency, and measure ad performance in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

---

## 2. Permissions Used by the App

The App requests the minimum permissions necessary for its intended functionality:
- **`INTERNET`**: Used to retrieve current and historical currency exchange rates from the Central Bank of Uzbekistan and load currency flag vectors.
- **`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`**: Used to determine if the device is connected to the internet, allowing the App to display cached offline rates when disconnected.
- **`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`**: Used exclusively to deliver user-configured rate alerts or daily currency notifications if explicitly enabled by the user. Notifications can be disabled at any time in the App settings or device settings.
- **`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`**: Required by Google Play Services for ad personalization and measurement via Google AdMob.
