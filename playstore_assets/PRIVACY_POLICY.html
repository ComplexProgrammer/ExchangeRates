Privacy Policy

Application: Valyuta Kurslari: O'zbekiston (exchangerates.complexprogrammer.uz)
Last updated: October 17, 2026

Complex Programmer ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile application Valyuta Kurslari: O'zbekiston (hereinafter referred to as the "App").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

We respect your privacy. The App does NOT collect any personal financial credentials, banking logins, names, or contact lists.

1. Information Collection and Use

The App is an informational and calculation utility that displays official currency exchange rates published by the Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

  • Personal Data: We do not ask for or collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use the App.
  • Advertising Identifier: The App uses the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) provided by Google Play Services to serve and personalize advertisements through Google AdMob, limit duplicate ads, and combat ad fraud.
  • Diagnostic and Performance Data: Anonymous diagnostic information, device model, operating system version, and crash logs may be collected through Google Firebase Crashlytics to monitor performance and resolve technical bugs.

2. Permissions Requested

  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to fetch official rates and verify connectivity.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Optional permission used solely to deliver custom rate alerts or daily summaries when requested by the user.
  • Ad ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Used by Google Play Services for standard AdMob advertisement operations.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

The App integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The App is developed independently by Complex Programmer and is NOT an official app of, endorsed by, or representing the Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan or any government agency. All exchange rates are retrieved from public open data sources:
